Guide

How to look

  1. 1 · Face the window

    Allow camera access and stand a few steps back so the whole window is on screen.

  2. 2 · Line up the frame

    Drag to move the frame and pinch (or scroll) to resize it until the corners sit on the window's corners. Use the faint outline as a guide.

  3. 3 · Window detected

    The corners turn orange and lock on. Motion and Product unlock, and the frame follows the window as you move.

  4. Audio

    The soundscape starts on its own. Tap to mute or unmute.

  5. Motion

    The saucer hovers, the beam lifts the sandals and stars glimmer.

  6. Product

    Pulsing dots mark each Hermès piece. Tap a dot to highlight its name.
